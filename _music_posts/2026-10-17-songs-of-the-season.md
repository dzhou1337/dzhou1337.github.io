# Fresh Beats of October 2026
#### 2026/10/17

My charming, discerning reader, I know not how you consume music. But I simply add songs to my Spotify Liked Songs playlist and start from the top most days, shuffling other days. This means I listen to the same songs a lot day-to-day, if not week-to-week. It really just depends on how many songs I've added recently. Let's walk through some highlights from the past month.

### less - Olivia Rodrigo
I'm pretty late to the train as I've only just started exploring Olivia Rodrigo's discography, and this is the first of her songs I've put on the liked list. The melancholic lyrics and simple instrumentation really capture this crushing and lonely feeling, one of longing to preserve something that deep-down you know you need to release. Gripping stuff.
