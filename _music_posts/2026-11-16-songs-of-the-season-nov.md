#### 2026/11/16

November time music time. Here's some of my favorite songs I discovered as our trees lost their leaves.

### Texas Meskin - That Mexican OT
I can't roll my R's and this guy is rolling consonants you've never even heard of. There's a real percussive quality to OT's voice like he has a snare drum in his mouth. Big recommend for any fans of latin hip hop with heavy merengue sampling

### city time - hanbee
J-pop vibes with dreamy vocals and upbeat instrumentation. There's a lot going on in the bass and the rhythm guitar but it somehow doesn't feel overly busy. Fun song to listen to while out on the town.
