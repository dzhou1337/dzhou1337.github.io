The Oakland A's broke everyone's minds so hard that the beautiful ballplayers who were overvalued in the early 2000s are now the undervalued true Moneyball darlings. Looking at this year's World Series matchup, we have two iconic Moneyball (read: poor) teams: the Tampa Bay Rays and the Milwaukee Brewers. These guys are playing beautiful baseball. They bunt and steal more than any other team. They haven't completely eschewed power and on-base skills, but they aren't completely optimized towards those skill sets like before. The Rays are starting Chandler Simpson, a guy who spent the 2026 season hitting 0 home runs, stealing 46 bases, and batting .306. Not since 1999 has a player batted over .300 with zero home runs throughout a complete season (Luis Castillo).

Moneyball was never about finding the optimal strategy for building a baseball team, it was about finding the optimal strategy for building a baseball team given strict budgetary constraints.
